Research briefing

What is what. The table and the numeric strip on each card are computed from cached price bars — deterministic, reproducible, and correct by construction. The written notes are language-model output produced at the timestamp shown on each card; they are a reading aid, they are not evidence, and nothing here feeds the backtest or the paper broker. When a note and the numbers disagree, believe the numbers.
